03 — Something sweet to finish

Chocolate Pudding Cookies

Discover the Recipe

A little sweetness goes a long way

Nadia's Childhood Favorite

There is something special about ending a meal with a dessert. Chocolate fudge pudding mix • Makes about 7 dozen.

Gather your ingredients

What You'll Need

  • 2¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • ¾ cup light brown sugar, firmly packed
  • ¼ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 package instant chocolate fudge pudding mix (4-serving size)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 eggs
  • 12 ounces chocolate chips

Made with love

How to Make Them

01

Preparation

Gather your ingredients and preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

02

Mix the Dry Ingredients

In a small bowl, mix together the flour and baking soda.

03

Mix the Wet Ingredients

In a separate, larger bowl, combine the softened butter, brown sugar, granulated sugar, dry pudding mix, and vanilla. Beat until smooth and creamy.

04

Combine

Beat in the eggs. Gradually add the flour mixture, then stir in the chocolate chips. The dough will be stiff.

05

Shape the Dough

Drop the dough by teaspoonfuls onto ungreased baking sheets, spacing the cookies about 2 inches apart.

05

Baking Time!

Bake for 8 to 10 minutes
*If you used larger eggs, then bake for 9 to 11 minutes.
