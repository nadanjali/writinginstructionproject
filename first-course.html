Arroz con Gandules

Explore the Recipe

A Taste of Home

Dahlia's Arroz con Gandules

My grandma made arroz con gandules all the time when I was growing up, and the smell of sofrito hitting a hot pan still means home to me. Now that I’m at school, this is the dish I make when I miss her kitchen. • Makes 6 servings

What You'll Need

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 ounces smoked ham, diced
  • 0.5 cups sofrito
  • 1 tablespoons sazón seasoning
  • 2 tablespoons tomato sauce
  • 20.3 cups pimento-stuffed olives, sliced
  • 15 ounces pigeon peas, canned, drained
  • 2 cups medium-grain white rice
  • 2.5 cups chicken broth or water
  • 1 teaspoons salt
  • 1 teaspoons dried oregano
  • 1 bay leaf

Time to Cook

Instructions

  1. Build the Base Heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil in a heavy pot (caldero or Dutch oven) over medium heat. Add 4 ounces smoked ham, diced and cook until lightly browned.
  2. Cook the Sofrito Stir in 0.5 cups sofrito, 1 tablespoon sazón seasoning, 1 teaspoon dried oregano, and 2 tablespoons tomato sauce. Cook until fragrant and slightly darkened.
  3. Toast the Rice Add 2 cups medium-grain white rice, 15 ounces pigeon peas, canned, drained, and 0.3 cups pimento-stuffed olives, sliced. Stir for a couple of minutes so the rice is coated in the sofrito mixture.
  4. Add Liquid and Boil Pour in 2.5 cups chicken broth or water and add 1 teaspoon salt. Stir once, then let it boil uncovered until the liquid drops level with the rice.
  5. Steam overed Stir once from the bottom, drop in 1 bay leaf, cover tightly, and reduce heat to low. Do not lift the lid.
  6. Rest and Serve Turn off the heat and rest, covered. Remove the bay leaf, fluff, and scoop up the crispy bottom layer (pegao) to serve on top.
