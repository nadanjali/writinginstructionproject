Chicken Quesadillas

Discover the Recipe

Simple ingredients, wonderful company

Bridget's Comforting Classic

Quesadillas are an easy dinner-party choice. They're warm, cheesy, customizable, and simple to prepare.

What you'll need

Ingredients

  • 10 inch tortillas
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • Rotisserie chicken, shredded
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 1 cup black beans (optional)
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Salsa, sour cream, or guacamole for serving

Let's get cooking

Instructions

  1. Preparation

    Gather all ingredients and cooking utensils, including a nonstick frying pan, spatula, and plate.

  2. Prep the Pan

    Lightly coat the frying pan with cooking spray to prevent the tortilla from sticking. Place the pan on the stove over medium heat.

  3. Assemble the Quesadillas

    Place a tortilla in a clean skillet. Sprinkle cheese over one half, then add the vegetables and chicken and optional black beans. Fold the tortilla in half.

  4. Cook Until Golden

    Allow the folded tortilla to cook for approximately 2–3 minutes, or until the bottom becomes golden brown and the cheese begins to melt.

  5. Flip and Finish Cooking

    Using a spatula, carefully flip the quesadilla. Cook the opposite side for another 2–3 minutes, until golden brown and the cheese is fully melted.

  6. Plate and Enjoy!

    Remove the quesadilla from the pan and place it on a plate. Allow it to cool briefly before cutting it into triangles. Serve with salsa or sour cream, and enjoy your quick, budget-friendly meal!
